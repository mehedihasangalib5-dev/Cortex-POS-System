Pricing FAQs

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?

No. You can start your 14-day free trial with just an email — no card required. We only ask for billing details if you choose a paid plan.

What happens after my 14-day trial ends?

Your dashboard locks until you choose a paid plan. Your data stays safe and unlocks immediately after you upgrade.

Can I switch between Starter and Pro later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Do you offer a discount for yearly billing?

Yes, switch to yearly billing above and save 20% compared to paying monthly.

Is support included in every plan?

Yes. Every plan, including the free trial, includes email support. Pro and Enterprise get priority support.
